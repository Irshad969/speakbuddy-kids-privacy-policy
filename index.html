Privacy Policy for SpeakBuddy Kids

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to SpeakBuddy Kids. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, educational environment for children. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our application.

1. Information Collection and Use

SpeakBuddy Kids is designed with privacy as a top priority.

  • No Account Required: We do not require users to create an account, nor do we collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Local Storage: All learning progress, XP (Experience Points), streaks, and unlocked mini-games are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to any external servers.

2. Microphone Access and Speech Recognition

To provide core educational features, SpeakBuddy Kids requires access to your device's microphone.

  • Why we need it: The microphone is used exclusively for the interactive voice learning features (e.g., validating pronunciation during Alphabet Adventure and Numbers Playground).
  • How it is processed:
    • The app utilizes the device's native Speech-to-Text services (provided by Google on Android devices and Apple on iOS devices).
    • The voice data captured by the microphone is processed by these native services to convert speech into text for validation.
    • No voice recordings are saved, stored, or transmitted to SpeakBuddy servers. Voice processing is handled entirely by the OS-level speech recognition engine according to Google/Apple's own privacy policies.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent any user data.

  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or data brokers.
  • No Advertisements: SpeakBuddy Kids is a completely ad-free experience, ensuring children are not exposed to external content or tracking cookies.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SpeakBuddy Kids is built specifically for children. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All educational interactions and voice validations happen locally or via the device's secure native speech APIs.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
